## Recommended bounded slice

Keep one wheat crop and one tractor. Add `till()`, `sow("wheat")`, `water()`, `attach("cultivator" | "seeder" | "waterer" | "harvester")` and `restock()`. Use the architecture's `sow` name for planting; do not introduce a second `plant` alias. Retain existing movement/harvest/depot APIs and Runtime-only `wait(n)`.

Use one equipped implement, changed only at a depot. A starter depot offers four permanently owned kits; kits are durable and not consumable stock. Attachment has duration/fuel cost but no purchase price in this slice. Do not model a scarce shared equipment pool until fleet/economy work. Inventory capacities survive implement changes, so switching cannot discard seed or water. Physical implement changes remain meaningful route-planning work.

Add bounded independent `Seeds/SeedCapacity` and `Water/WaterCapacity` to tractors. Keep cargo exclusively harvested wheat. Depot gains `SeedStock/SeedCapacity` and `WaterStock/WaterCapacity`, plus an immutable allowlisted kit catalog (at most four entries in this slice). Seeds and water come from visible finite depot stocks. There is no seed reproduction, contract currency or automatic stock refill yet; a second cycle is possible while stocks/fuel remain. Describe this limit in the reference and inspector. Resource replenishment/economy is a later decision, not an implicit infinite-resource mode.

Maintain grid <=64x64, actors <=16, resources/capacities <=1,000,000, action durations 1..10,000 and existing source/runtime/save budgets. No weather RNG, crop death, multi-crop cargo, irrigation network, fleet queues, automatic navigation or new syntax is needed.

## Rules and state transitions

Proposed starter balance is content data, subject to playtesting: maturity 10 growth units; eligible growth +1/tick; seed cost 1 per sow; watering costs 10 water units and adds 10 moisture units; moisture capacity 1,000,000; one moisture unit consumed per eligible growth increment; harvest yield 1 wheat. Till/sow/water each take 2 ticks and cost 1 fuel; attach/restock take 1 tick and cost 0. Existing movement/turn/harvest costs remain 1; unload/refuel cost 0. Validate that watering can support at least one whole maturity cycle and reject invalid/overflowing rates before creating a world.

For the new rules only, planted wheat requires moisture to grow. At each growth phase, if wheat is immature, was present at tick start, and moisture >= configured per-tick cost, deduct that cost and add the configured integer growth rate, saturating at maturity. Mature wheat consumes no moisture. Dry wheat pauses without dying or losing progress. Empty tiles do not lose moisture. No evaporation yet. Water completion is effective during that tick's growth phase for an existing crop; a crop sown in that same completion phase starts growth on the next tick. These conventions must be covered by exact trace tests.

| Action | Tick-start validation/reservation | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Till | Cultivator equipped; current tile unblocked, empty and untilled; sufficient fuel; reserve tile. | Set tilled=true; preserve moisture; no crop/material output. |
| Sow wheat | Seeder equipped; tile tilled and empty; one available seed; fuel; reserve seed and tile. | Set crop=Wheat, growth=0; consume reserved seed; preserve moisture. |
| Water | Waterer equipped; tile tilled (empty or planted); room for the complete configured moisture addition; enough water/fuel; reserve water and tile. | Add the full moisture quantity; consume water. No partial watering or overflow. |
| Attach | At depot; requested kit in its owned catalog; fuel; reserve actor's equipment transition. | Replace equipped kit atomically; old kit is still owned by depot catalog; supplies/cargo unchanged. |
| Restock | At depot; enough stock to fill both supply compartments; fuel; reserve both deficits from depot stocks. | Fill both capacities after consuming configured action fuel. If either stock is short, reject the entire request. Already-full compartments have zero deficit. |

Harvest still requires a harvester, ripe wheat and full yield cargo room. Completion clears crop/growth, sets tilled=false, and preserves remaining moisture in new-cycle rules. The next crop requires till again. Legacy v1 harvest preserves its existing tilled behavior. Reject till on a crop (`crop_present`) or already-tilled soil (`already_tilled`); sow on untilled soil (`soil_not_tilled`), without seed (`insufficient_seed`), or on a crop (`crop_present`); water without stock (`insufficient_water`) or addition capacity (`moisture_full`); attachment to an unavailable owned kit (`implement_unavailable`). Preserve existing common fuel/busy/depot reasons and add named lower-case constants for new reasons.

The new moisture capacity and costs belong to validated rules. Require moisture capacity <=1,000,000, positive water addition/cost and seed cost, positive eligible growth rate/cost, maturity/yield >0, and seed cost <= configured starter compartment capacity. Use checked wide arithmetic for sums and transfer deficits. Do not infer a maturity threshold or a refund from presentation animation.

## Reservation and cancellation accounting

Keep one pending action per actor, origin occupancy and existing stable round-robin arbitration. New tile operations target the tractor's current tile and reserve it until completion; no actor can move or attach concurrently. Movement checks prevent another tractor occupying a reserved operation tile. Validation uses state t; effects apply exactly once at completion t+d.

At acceptance, move action fuel/seed/water out of available tractor quantities into reservation. For Restock, deduct seed/water transfer amounts from available depot stocks; reserve destination compartment room. Till/Sow/Water do not mutate soil/crop/moisture at acceptance; Attach does not replace equipment early. Harvest cargo reservations and all-or-nothing unload/refuel rules remain intact.

Stop refunds unused fuel and tractor seed/water, returns uncompleted Restock stock to its source depot, releases all tile/capacity/equipment reservations and emits one cancelled result per active action. Repeated Stop cannot refund again. Completed sow/till/water/attach/restock remains applied. Natural growth/moisture already consumed on previous ticks is not rolled back by cancelling an action. Stop itself advances neither growth nor time. Crop/material effects may not be reconstructed from logs or animation.

Conservation tests use available+reserved seed/water across tractor and depot; completed sow consumes SeedCost, completed watering consumes WaterCost and creates the configured tile moisture, and eligible growth consumes moisture. Fuel decreases only by completed action costs; cancellation restores unused reservations. Restock/attach only transfer/change equipment and do not create resources. Validation/rejection creates none and loses none.

## Campaign reachability and teaching

Leave current chapters 0/1 and their v1 policies unchanged during the save/reference milestone. New campaign content needs a reviewed chapter model beyond the current hardcoded 0/1 DTO/policy; it must not silently fall into the current "any later chapter gets counted loops" behavior.

Recommend a separate chapter 2, "Read and restore the field", granting assignment/arithmetic, conditions and while on entry along with till/sow/water/attach/restock and documented sensor fields. Functions remain unavailable; the architecture's future "Reusable routines" chapter is deferred until functions actually exist. Sandbox grants every newly implemented API with identical safety limits. Unlock validation and gateway checks both remain required.

A reachability fixture can use home depot (0,0), one empty tile (1,0), east-facing tractor, 50 fuel capacity, cargo capacity 8, seed capacity 4, water capacity 40, and depot stocks of 8 seeds/80 water/500 fuel. Chapter entry provides the four owned kits and supplies through explicit versioned content, once per chapter entry recorded in progress. No hidden grants on Run, reload or failed command.

A valid first-cycle trace starts at depot: restock; attach cultivator; travel to target, till and return; attach seeder; travel, sow and return; attach waterer; travel, water and return; attach harvester; travel, wait/sense until ripe, harvest and return; unload. Each out-and-back is two moves plus four right turns. Four trips cost 24 fuel; till+sow+water+harvest cost 4 more, for total 28. One seed and 10 water are consumed. Starting at 50 leaves 22 fuel; refuel before repeating reserves 28 from the displayed depot stock. A second cycle consumes another seed/10 water and fits capacities/stocks. Content tests derive this budget from configured rules; 28 is fixture evidence, not a production special case.

Complete the chapter only from authoritative completed actions in the correct crop lineage: till -> sow -> water -> harvest -> nonempty unload of the grown crop. Harvesting the old starter field, empty unload, existing depot wheat or a rejected/cancelled action must not satisfy it. Recommended Application objective state is a bounded per-tile stage/generation marker and per-actor count of qualifying harvested wheat still in cargo; increment it only for an event matching that completed cycle and discharge it on that actor's successful all-cargo unload. Validate qualifying counts <= actual wheat cargo. A tile's successful Till prepares its next Sow generation; only Water after that Sow counts. Per-tile generation is Domain state, while teaching-stage/qualifying-credit state is Application progress; define their serialization together before implementation. No general multi-crop cargo provenance framework is needed for this single-crop objective. Scalar wheat totals alone cannot prove this chain. Preserve partial objective progress in v2 stopped saves so reloading neither awards completion nor loses a valid cycle. Do not impose fuel medals or an exact prescribed route for basic completion.

Players may waste all fuel/seeds through failed programs, so a valid trace alone does not prove recovery. Recommend an explicit confirmed retry from a retained chapter-entry checkpoint, preserving all draft scripts and prior chapter progress. Store one bounded checkpoint world and its compatibility identity in v2 session data (never interpreter stacks); validate total envelope size including both worlds. Entry grants must not reapply after reload, and retry must not farm completion rewards. Without checkpoint/recovery or an explicit future economy recovery rule, campaign nonblocking reachability is not satisfied. UI work needs a separate Experience delegation; this proposal does not implement a retry control.

## v1 save compatibility and versioning

Keep the envelope's framing/checksum/strict JSON and size budgets. Recommend envelope schemaVersion=1, new worldSchemaVersion=2 and sessionSchemaVersion=2 for the extended authoritative fields and objective/checkpoint state. Assign new simulation/content/rules IDs such as `harvest-cycle-v2`, `first-cycle-v2`, `cycle-v2`; names are proposals, not current constants. Stop before save; pending actions/continuations are never encoded. Validate full rule values as well as IDs, all stocks/capacities, kit catalogs, crop lineage/progress and checkpoint references before live replacement.

Do not add new keys to v1, change the meaning of its existing moisture, or pretend its current codec accepts omitted future fields. Current StrictJson rejects unexpected/missing structural keys; rule matching would reject changed growth semantics even if the rules version string were reused.

Recommended compatibility policy: retain a strict legacy v1 reader and explicitly selected legacy rule profile, so existing farms keep their unconditional growth, old till behavior, stocks/scripts/chapter and existing replay hashes. Legacy sessions expose only supported legacy APIs. Their validator must retain the exact v1 enum/field/rule subset after future enums expand; nonzero new supplies, a new implement, or cycle semantics cannot be emitted as a legacy v1 save. Keep v1's original tile/resource bounds rather than retroactively applying new-cycle invariants. The save/reference milestone can ship entirely on this profile. New-cycle farms use v2 and write v2; an old executable rejects v2 world/session schema instead of partially loading it. Compatibility routing must select a known registered rules/content profile before decoding; never trust rule values in a save to enable an unknown profile.

Do not automatically upgrade v1 farms. An optional later import must be an explicit validated conversion to a distinct v2 slot, keeping the v1 source/backup untouched. A concrete conversion proposal is: preserve IDs, tick, RNG, cursor, processed IDs, existing wheat/cargo/depot/fuel/scripts; preserve tilled flags; preserve moisture and give immature crops a declared one-time finite top-up only if needed for their remaining growth (default rules: max(0, 10 - growth - existingMoisture)); record bounded starting supply capacities/stocks and kit ownership as visible import grants; map chapter 0/1 unchanged and initialize new lineage/progress as empty. Never claim previously loaded starter crop satisfies the new sow objective. Record conversion version/receipt in v2; repeat imports into new slots cannot award campaign rewards. This conversion changes semantics/hash, so compare pre-conversion v1 replay only under legacy rules and start a new v2 replay baseline afterward.

Implementation of import/grants is deferred unless Chief chooses it. A dual-profile reader is already coordinated work: Domain legacy/new rule behavior, Persistence explicit codecs and compatibility registry, Application profile selection/policy and transactional restore, Composition content registration. The existing AtomicSlotStore cannot simply validate an old-profile primary with a new-profile codec and replace it; preserve legacy slots/recovery candidates and wire per-profile validation. Continue explicit recovery selection, full revalidation and mode directory separation; no automatic temp promotion or overwrite of unsupported future saves.
